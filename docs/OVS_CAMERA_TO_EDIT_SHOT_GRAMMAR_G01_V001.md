# OVS-CAMERA-TO-EDIT-SHOT-G01 V001 — Shot Grammar / Transition Interface
**Status:** PROPOSED / review branch only. **Existing V003 transition registry remains 200 techniques, 16 families.**

## Purpose
Before a transition is placed in an edit, define both participating camera shots so the matching decision has source-authenticated evidence. This prevents postproduction from forcing continuity on incompatible subject positions, lens perspectives and motion phases.

## 12 OVS shot-grammar classes
These are **studio-synthesized workflow classes**, drawn from cinematography practice and observed reference examples; not a verbatim transcription of the tutorial:
1. SG-01 Environmental establishing / extreme wide — confirm geography.
2. SG-02 Spatial master / scene view — reveal blocking and room relationships.
3. SG-03 Full figure / cowboy — retain body gesture and context.
4. SG-04 Medium single — observable performer behavior.
5. SG-05 Tight single / close-up — emotional detail and eyeline.
6. SG-06 Extreme close-up — eye/object/form feature for matching.
7. SG-07 Insert / prop detail — causal evidence and physical contact.
8. SG-08 Two-shot / relationship — spacing, power, negative space.
9. SG-09 OTS / dirty single — relational vantage, camera-side axis.
10. SG-10 POV / subjective view — knowledge and perceptual contract.
11. SG-11 High/low/overhead vantage — motivated perspective and scale.
12. SG-12 Moving reveal / tracking — motion vector, parallax, practical occlusion.

## Shot-edge mandatory record
Source filename and SHA-256 (private, not in a public GitHub document); actual in/out timecodes and FPS; script revision and dramatic/musical beat; shot grammar class; performer identity and performance authority; actor/prop/body and wardrobe state; focal length and camera orientation/height; framing anchor position normalized 0-1; outgoing and incoming movement vector and phase; visual color/exposure/gain and grain; sonic cue, incoming prelap, room tone and rights; A clean/B match/C design version mapping; all immersion/flash QC; independent and founder selection.

## Editorial choice law
- First build and score the **clean-cut baseline**, not an effect.
- A visual match chain requires pairwise match quality **and** sequence-wide setup/variation/payoff.
- Sound match or J/L cut can outperform visible designs where the audience needs sustained performance.
- Designed optics, collage and AI morphing demand a specific dramatic rationale and independent identity/continuity checks.
- Do not use reference tutorial scenes, music, logos or screenshots in production.
- Zero generation spend by default; source/identity locks and explicit human review remain separate.

## Qualification states
PROPOSED -> SOURCE VERIFIED -> CAMERA/ACTOR AUTHORITY VERIFIED -> EDITED A/B/C -> INDEPENDENT COLD READ -> FOUNDER SELECT -> PROJECT-SPECIFIC ADOPTION. The G01D.3 device-mount hold blocks transitions to source-verified for the garage test.
