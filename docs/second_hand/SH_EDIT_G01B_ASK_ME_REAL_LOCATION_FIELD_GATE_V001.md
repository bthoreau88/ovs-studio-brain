### Performance and story intent
Three adult performers at one authorized reception counter: Beni nearest counter, Thoreau half a step behind, attendant initially addressing Thoreau. The attendant asks whose name to place on room; Thoreau reflexively starts to answer for Beni, who interrupts: "You can ask me." Thoreau chooses not to speak further. Attendant redirects to Beni, and she supplies her name. Test dialogue is NOT screenplay-locked and requires practical motel check-in plausibility review.

Meaning is communicated by **attendant attention, Beni agency and Thoreau restraint**. Avoid horror slogans, illustrative superpowers, supernatural sound cues or morphing a face to manufacture a character event.

- Natural performance: one primary behavior and one secondary observable beat maximum. Avoid coaching Thoreau into a flourish or Beni into a slogan-like declaration.
