## 3. Preregistered participant procedure
1. Recruit **8–12** independent binocular viewers with no previous exposure to the A/B frames, annotated visuals or explanation.
2. Do not show the PDF/contact sheet or suggest there is a second person or camera motion.
3. Pre-screen familiarity. Anonymous code + device + portrait/eye condition. Avoid collecting identifying details.
4. Say only: **“You will see two images, one at a time, for four seconds each. Tell us what you saw.”**
5. Present A for 4s; hide; ask “How many people did you see?” Choices One / Two or more / Unsure.
6. Present B for 4s; hide; ask same person count. Then ask, verbatim: **“What, if anything, seems different between the two pictures?”**
7. Export untouched anonymous JSON, timestamp and SHA-256. No auto-interpretation. Any previously exposed/interrupted/incorrectly timed trial is documented and excluded from the primary denominator.
8. Two independent human coders record YES/NO for revised interpretation, two separate persons, and camera/viewpoint attribution. Unresolved disagreement => HOLD.
9. **Internal screening only**: >=60% revised interpretation and >=70% explicitly identifying **both independent people and camera/viewpoint change**. These are not scientific significance or conversion rates. Must have >=8 eligible completed binocular trials before calculating.
10. Optional one-eye trial results are analyzed separately; closing one eye does not uncover hidden pixels on an unchanging phone image.
