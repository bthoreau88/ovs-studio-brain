# SH-PROMO-ILLUSION-G01A.3 — Pre-registered independent cold-read protocol V001
**Registered 2026-11-09 PT before enrollment. No observed human responses.**

## Method
- Primary 8–12 independent, *previously unexposed* binocular viewers. Do not pool optional monocular trials. No medical/biometric data; anonymous trial codes only.
- Display exact original G01A.2 repaired 1080×1920 plate A for 4 seconds, hide and ask “How many people did you see?” One / Two or more / Unsure.
- Display exact repaired plate B for 4 seconds, hide and repeat count. Then neutrally ask “What, if anything, seems different between the two pictures?”
- Do not pre-cue second-person, mirror, trick, optical illusion or camera movement. Do not give the participant an annotated proof sheet. Do not replay.
- Save raw anonymous JSON unaltered, preserve SHA-256; allow withdrawal or document interruption. No remote telemetry needed.
- Two independent human reviewers (unique reviewer_code) examine verbatim response **separately**. Record yes/no for: revision of first interpretation; two separately present persons; camera/viewpoint change; morph/teleport misinterpretation. Reviewer disagreement or missing values => HOLD pending human adjudication.
- HTML and scoring scripts may be executed locally from separately delivered ZIP. They are not hosted in the public GitHub repo and contain no respondent data.

## Internal screening conditions only
Minimum 8 eligible independent binocular trials, complete raw logs, valid stimulus hashes, no duplicate trial IDs, no unaddressed exclusion or inter-rater disagreement. 60% or more should report revised interpretation. 70% or more must independently identify both **two separate bodies** and **changed camera/viewpoint**. Do not confuse the separate initial A leakage proportion with comprehension. No claim of inferential statistical significance or viral performance. A null result may reflect the use of two stills rather than a continuous shot; cannot be interpreted as evidence for video efficacy.

## Data privacy
No participant JSON committed to public GitHub. Facilitator retains raw unaltered bytes in local private storage, shares only authorized aggregate/de-identified findings when permitted. Subjects can choose not to export.
