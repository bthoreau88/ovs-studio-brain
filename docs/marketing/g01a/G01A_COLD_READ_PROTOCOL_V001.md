# G01A.1 Independent Cold-Read Protocol V001
**SH-PROMO-ILLUSION-G01A / THE OTHER ANGLE**

## Research question
Does an audience initially register one person in the pre-dolly static geometry still, then register two independently present people in the end still? This is an exploratory design assessment, **not** a clinical or scientific study of stereopsis.

## Required set
- Use the unannotated 0 cm still A and the unannotated 40 cm still B from the local G01A ZIP, or the included self-contained offline blind-read HTML.
- 8–12 independent viewers; record anonymous code, device class and binocular/monocular viewing condition. Phone portrait orientation preferred; do not mix in technical illustrations before the read.
- Keep camera/render source identical for all participants. Do not let viewers confer.
- Viewers with visual accessibility needs can skip, use an alternate condition or receive verbal scene descriptions. Log a noncomparable test if instructions were modified.
- No biometric, health or identity data needed. The offline page records no remote analytics and downloads only a CSV when the tester asks.

## Sequence
1. Tell participant: "You will see an image briefly. Answer what you saw." No words about two people, occlusion, the Mimic or optical illusions.
2. Show A for **4 seconds**, then hide it. Ask: "How many people did you see?" Choices one / two or more / unsure. Record raw first response.
3. Show B for **4 seconds** in the same 9:16 layout. Hide. Ask again. Record raw second response.
4. Ask open-ended: "What physically changed between the two views?" Record verbatim in free text.
5. If doing a one-eye condition, say only "For this trial, view using one eye." Do not suggest it makes the image change or newly visible pixels appear.
6. Offer optional debrief only **after** all answers: a lateral camera move reveals another stationary person.

## Operational scoring
- Prior G01 exploratory threshold A: >=60% of independent respondents express a revised initial interpretation.
- Threshold B: >=70% correctly state that there were **two physically present people** and a **change of camera viewpoint**, not actor teleportation/morphing.
- With n=8, threshold A requires at least 5 and B at least 6 qualifying respondents; with n=12, threshold A at least 8 and B at least 9. These are round-up operational checks and are not statistical validation.
- Score by open-ended causal explanation before revealing the mechanism to the viewer.
- Log uncertain, excluded, abandoned or altered-condition responses; do not silently remove them.

## Failure modes and disposition
- If A already shows two people to most participants, adjust alignment/proximity rather than scripting an unsupported claim.
- If B is interpreted as a morph, apparition or digital effect, revisit blocking and rendered depth cues.
- If viewing with one eye is required for the effect to work, reject this flat-screen Reel mechanism; it must remain legible binocularly and mute.
- Record explicit founder PASS / REVISE / REJECT **after** real survey data and independently verified source shot/location become available.
