### 1. The creative proposition

Build a psychologically meaningful 15-second promotional illusion from **two independently present people who initially appear to occupy one silhouette**. A lateral camera move reveals that one person was physically behind the other. The Mimic may adopt a small, delayed human behavior, and Beni may visibly orient toward this separate presence. No identity morphing or false supernatural causation. The physical geometry, not an algorithmic edit, creates the reveal.

**Truth rule:** closing one eye cannot reveal hidden pixels in an unchanged flat phone-screen image. Monocular viewing may alter subjective depth, but the hero Reel must work with both eyes and audio muted. The reveal is produced by controlled camera parallax. A genuinely angle-dependent print could be a separate mechanically tested physical artifact.

### 5. Image, lens and sound style

- **Scene:** Dellwood Motel, Room 14, plausible worn interior surfaces.
- **Camera response:** ARRI Alexa 35 character; 40 mm scene and ~65–85 mm close-up, Cooke S4/i or Panchro/i-type restrained falloff.
- **Lights:** tungsten practical frame left; subdued cyan/teal exterior spill frame right; deep negative fill; realistic human complexion.
- **Texture:** 35 mm fine grain with restrained halation; no plastic skin, beautification or fake analog glitches.
- **Placement:** physically separate foreground and background planes, accurate independent shadows, realistic contact and sight lines.
- **Sound:** air/hum/cloth tension; optional 300–500 ms removal of a steady ambiance layer during reveal; no jumpscare or predictive speech. Must work muted.
- **Text:** render typography outside generated plate; original verified OVS logo and approved title only.
- **Image/film continuity:** no multilayer poster grid within camera footage, no impossible silhouette merging, no 3D HUD.

### 7. Prompt registry (design-only; NOT run authority)

**P01 — Architectural base plate.**
Create a 9:16 cinematic marketing image inside Room 14. Locked 40 mm shoulder-height camera, two independent depth planes, tungsten left and teal right, deep negative fill, aged set surfaces, restrained fine-grain film response. Two fully separate people are partly aligned by ordinary occlusion; no transformation or text rendered within the image. Film canon unchanged.

**P02 — Aligned state A.**
Preserve approved architectural plate exactly. Thoreau near camera, a separate actor several feet behind; the outlines align from this camera station but anatomy never merges. Apply the separately approved identity sources; keep physically distinct shadows. No new props.

**P03 — Revealed state B.**
Repeat plate A without moving either performer or any set/wardrobe/light object. Translate camera laterally 25–45 cm and recalculate natural parallax, occlusion and focus. The rear performer becomes visibly separate. Nothing has appeared, disappeared or transformed.

**P04 — Beni reaction.**
65–85 mm close-up: Beni recognizes the independently present performer offscreen, orients her gaze and holds one incomplete breath. Preserve approved face geometry, finger-wave hairline, natural skin and body. The G02A outfit is optional pending separate approval; no overacting.

**P05 — Mimic micro-behavior.**
Use SH-MIMIC-G01 as identity source; pale natural asymmetry, wide unsettled eyes, weathered black clothing and loose wet-looking hair. Primary beat: watch; secondary: imitate a subtle shoulder-settling motion. No face swap, sudden re-skinning, object/hand trick or generic horror gesture.

**P06 — Neutral title plate.**
Warm-near-black film-textured background only; fine grain, restrained optical halation, compositing-safe. No generated lettering, logotype or false trademark.

**P07 — Motion planning prompt.**
For FUTURE, separately authorized testing: 2s locked two-actor shot, 3s controlled horizontal dolly 25–45 cm, 1s settle, preserving the two separate people and all source/identity geometry. No identity-morph transition, face warp or added set object. This written prompt does not authorize video generation.

**P08 — Website interaction.**
Three-plane, drag-to-change-viewpoint parallax demo; keyboard controls, visible slider, reset, reduced-motion pair and accessible text. No eye-based claim, hidden tracking permissions, hover-only dependencies or auto-sound.

**P09 — Physical card.**
Prototype an actually view-dependent offset-window or multi-layer/lenticular card. Register layers so viewpoint change physically separates independently printed silhouettes. Test at real viewing distances before any print procurement.

**P10 — Sound.**
Original low room hum, real environmental movement and subtle cloth tension; optional 300–500 ms ambient subtraction during reveal. Do not use reverse-prophecy recordings, evidence-media devices, tapping or jumpscare stings.

**P11 — Continuity adversarial QC.**
Compare pre/post frames: face ratios, iris, hairline, jewelry, glasses, textures, garment seams, room perspective, light, shadow, reflections and focal response. Report PASS / REPAIR / HOLD by exact frame. Ensure independent body presence all along.

**P12 — Perceptual truth test.**
Show at 9:16 phone size, muted, with two-eyed and one-eyed viewers. Ask number of people before and after reveal, and what physically changed. Reject misleading scientific copy, forced instructions and effects mistaken for AI morphing.
