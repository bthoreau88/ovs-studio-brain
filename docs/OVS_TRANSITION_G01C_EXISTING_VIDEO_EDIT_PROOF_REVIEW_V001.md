## Actual tests prepared

## Creative / governance findings

- The two source videos are **existing rendered alternate takes**, not authenticated camera-original footage. They do not establish an approved current film character identity or source authority.
- Hand gesture and eyeline are usable visual anchors for a chain test, but gaze direction, performance phase, face detail and framing differ.
- Version C deliberately implies two simultaneous views of the same subject and could create unintended story implications.
- The earlier library clip depicting retired SECOND HAND plot mechanics was explicitly excluded, not revived.
- Audio transition tests remain **NOT ASSESSED** because all proof exports are muted.
- The experiment does **not** verify a continuity pass, a narrative improvement, release clearances, or any project-canon eligibility.
- There were no paid generation credits, no new generative shots, no source mutations and no publication changes.
